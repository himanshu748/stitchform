# Requirements: Stitchform landing page

Audience is an applicant or helper who needs to complete a paper form, and a judge who needs to understand and try the journey quickly. The page covers discovery and entry into the existing editor.

1. WHEN a visitor opens the root page, THE SYSTEM SHALL explain the difficulty of interpreting, answering and checking a paper form, and how Stitchform helps.
2. WHEN the visitor sees the example, THE SYSTEM SHALL label both the fictional form and its answers as sample data, and show a preview of an actual PDF export.
3. WHEN a visitor chooses their own form, THE SYSTEM SHALL open the existing form picker. WHEN they choose the scholarship example, THE SYSTEM SHALL open the built-in sample at field check.
4. IF the sample cannot load, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the form picker and a retry instruction.
5. THE SYSTEM SHALL describe local processing, typed English/Hindi answering and the opt-in boundary for microphone and model assistance.
6. THE SYSTEM SHALL support keyboard navigation and responsive layouts down to 320px. Form processing SHALL remain available directly at `/app/`.

From photo to filled form

  1. Capture and check

    Take a photo or upload an image. Stitchform looks for labels and boxes. Check and correct anything it missed before you start.

  2. Answer one field at a time

    Type in English or Hindi. You can also speak where your browser supports it. Review flagged dates, missing answers and signature reminders.

  3. Print or pick up later

    Download a PDF with your answers in place. Save a passphrase-protected stitch code if you want to refill text answers later.

Your form stays in your browser by default.

Stitchform reads images and builds PDFs on your device. No Stitchform server stores your answers.

If you turn on voice or model assistance, your browser or chosen provider may process audio or answer text. Stitchform asks before sending them.
